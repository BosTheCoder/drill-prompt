Mock interviewer

A prompt that turns ChatGPT into a strict interviewer. It runs a realistic timed round, pushes back the way a real one does, then writes a candid hire or no-hire review with a reference design.

  1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT, paste and send.
  2. Answer as you would out loud. Typing with dictation keeps its interview clock accurate; voice mode works too.
  3. When it asks for your diagram, paste a screenshot or copied Excalidraw shapes.
  4. Say "end interview" for the written review.
